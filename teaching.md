# Teaching & Mentoring

## Mentoring

I mentor women in IT & Data Science through [coffeecodebreak](https://coffeecodebreak.de/html/female-tech-mentors-en.html) — supporting mentees navigating technical career paths, from breaking into the field to growing into senior roles.

## Workshops & Guest Lectures

### Machine Learning: Advanced Data Analysis Concepts with Python
*Since 2020*

[Workshop](https://scientifica.de/bildungsangebote/informatica-feminale-bw/informatica-feminale-bw-2021/if-kurse-2021/detailansicht/event/21if-21-hw2-machine-learning-advanced-data-analysis-concepts-with-python-english/) at the summer school [Informatica Feminale](https://scientifica.de/bildungsangebote/informatica-feminale-bw/informatica-feminale/), University of Freiburg / University of Furtwangen, Germany.

Hands-on introduction to supervised and unsupervised machine learning — regression, classification, dimensional reduction — alongside the statistical principles behind them, model interpretability, and applied use of open-source libraries.

### Data Analysis Concepts with Python
*Since 2019*

[Workshop](https://scientifica.de/bildungsangebote/informatica-feminale-bw/informatica-feminale-bw-2021/if-kurse-2021/detailansicht/event/21if-02-hw1-data-analysis-concepts-with-python-english/) at the summer school [Informatica Feminale](https://scientifica.de/bildungsangebote/informatica-feminale-bw/informatica-feminale/), University of Freiburg / University of Furtwangen, Germany.

Covers the full data analysis workflow in Python: efficient handling of large datasets, explorative/descriptive/inferential analysis, the statistics behind common methods, and visualization of complex data.

### Uncertainty in Climate Data Across Scales
*2021*

Module 3 of the interdisciplinary summer school [*Trends, Rhythms and Events in the Earth's Climate System*](http://mres.uni-potsdam.de/index.php/2020/09/18/summer-school-on-trends-rhythms-and-events-in-the-earths-climate-system-past-present-and-future/#more-3730), University of Potsdam, funded by the Volkswagen Foundation.

Fundamentals of uncertainty in climate science — from sources of uncertainty in proxy-based reconstructions to frequentist and Bayesian approaches for predictive climate data analysis.

### Earth Surface Dynamics — Understanding Processes at the Earth's Vulnerable Skin
*2018*

Summer school module at the University of Potsdam, funded by the Volkswagen Foundation, for 25 doctoral students from geosciences, environmental sciences and related fields.

Covered signal and noise in Earth-surface processes, non-destructive surveying methods, and modelling the underlying processes shaping the data.
