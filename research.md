# Research

My research has consistently revolved around the question:
> **How can we extract reliable information from complex systems when observations are noisy, incomplete, uncertain, and scale-dependent?**

## 🔬 Topics

### ⏱️ Time series & dynamics

Modelling systems that evolve over time, including non-stationarity, structural changes, autocorrelation and uncertainty.

### 🎲 Bayesian & probabilistic modelling

Using probabilistic models to represent uncertainty explicitly rather than reducing every prediction to a single number.

### 🌐 Complex systems

Understanding interacting processes where simple local mechanisms can produce difficult-to-predict global behaviour.

### 📉 Uncertainty & sensitivity

Quantifying which assumptions and inputs matter, and understanding how uncertainty propagates through a model.

### 💻 Scientific computing

Building reproducible computational workflows that make statistical and mathematical methods useful on real-world data.

### 🤖 AI-assisted analytics & agentic systems

Applying LLMs and agentic architectures to extend — not replace — established analytical pipelines: synthesizing statistical output into readable reports, resolving free-text queries against structured data, and evaluating where time series foundation models genuinely help versus where classical methods still win.

## 🔁 From research to data science

The same principles remain useful outside academic research: understand the data-generating process, encode meaningful structure, quantify uncertainty, and validate models against the problem they are supposed to solve. That includes AI-based components — an LLM in a pipeline is still a model, and deserves the same scrutiny about what it actually improves.
