## Context

Time series foundation models promise to generalize forecasting across domains without task-specific training. Whether that promise holds up for BMW Group's own forecasting problems was worth a direct test.

## Approach

At a BMW Group × AWS hackathon, I led a time series forecasting use case evaluating Chronos against the team's existing methods. A direct exchange with the model's paper authors added context beyond the benchmark numbers.

## Outcome

Chronos did not outperform the team's existing approach for this use case. The exercise sharpened the team's understanding of where time series LLMs currently help — and where they don't — informing forecasting strategy beyond the hackathon itself.

**Stack:** Chronos, Python
